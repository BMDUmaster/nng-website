How it works

From your question to an appointment

An enquiry starts a conversation with the team. It does not book or pay for a session.

  1. 01

    Share your question

    Tell the team what is on your mind and what you have tried.

  2. 02

    Confirm the details

    Agree on format, duration, availability and fee before booking. For online sessions, share your country and time zone.

  3. 03

    Meet Narayani

    Attend your appointment online or in person.

  4. 04

    Decide what comes next

    Discuss your next steps and whether ongoing guidance would suit you.
