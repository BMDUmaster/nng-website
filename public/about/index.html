Her story

The story behind her name and practice

In her book, Narayani shares the personal moments that shaped her sense of purpose.

  1. An early calling

    A spiritual calling, before the name Narayani.

    In her book, she recalls being known as Simmi in her early life, while feeling drawn towards spiritual work and service.

    A name given during her Vedic wedding ceremony.
