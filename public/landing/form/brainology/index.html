✦How Narayani approaches it

Notice the pattern.
Then train a new response.

A pattern is a small chain that repeats: a thought, a belief, the words you use and a reaction. Narayani helps you see the chain, and find the one link you can reach and practise differently.

The chain that repeats

  1. A thought
  2. A belief
  3. The words you use
  4. A reaction

  1. 01Name the problem

    We start with what’s actually going wrong.

  2. 02See the pattern

    We look at how your thoughts and beliefs lead to your usual response.

  3. 03Choose a different action

    We work out the next step that breaks the pattern.
