✦How Narayani approaches it

Notice the pattern.
Then train a new response.

A pattern is a small chain that repeats: a thought, a belief, the words you use and a reaction. Narayani helps you see the chain, and find the one link you can reach and practise differently.

The chain that repeats

  1. A thought
  2. A belief
  3. The words you use
  4. A reaction

  1. 01Notice

    Name one situation that keeps returning, and the first thought it brings.

  2. 02Understand

    Explore the belief, words and reaction that hold the pattern in place.

  3. 03Practise

    Take a simple daily practice into everyday life, and return to it.
