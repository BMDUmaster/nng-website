Questions

Before you enquire

A few things you may want to know first.

Something else on your mind?If you have any other questions, feel free to ask. We’re here to help.

Ask your question →
01How often will we speak?

The program includes direct guidance over six months. The team explains the rhythm and contact arrangements before you commit.

02Can my family be part of it?

Yes. Many questions involve a partner, children or parents, and the program can take them into account.

03Can I start with a single consultation?

Yes. You can begin with one consultation and decide about the program afterwards.

04Can I take part from outside India?

Online guidance is available outside India. Mention your country and time zone when you enquire.

05What does the program cost?

The fee depends on the format and is shared when you enquire, before you decide.

06Does this replace medical or professional advice?

No. This is personal and spiritual guidance. It does not replace medical care, mental health treatment, legal advice or financial advice from qualified professionals.
